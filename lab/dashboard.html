Loading system information...

Testing does not update the network. A test contains 15 tasks: three per bot. Training uses the examples already collected and leaves the bots waiting when it finishes.

What to expect

  1. Collect examplesThe teacher steers the bots toward goals. Their observations and actions become training examples. The network is not learning yet.
  2. Train the networkThe bots stop moving on purpose. The network studies your examples for 100 training passes, then saves a checkpoint.
  3. Test learned behaviorThe bots move using the trained network, without the teacher. Watch how many goals they reach.
  4. Review and repeatCompare the results. More examples may help, but a perfect score on this simple course calls for a harder task.

This first curriculum teaches flat-course navigation. It does not yet teach general survival.
