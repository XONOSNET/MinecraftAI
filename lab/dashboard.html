Autonomous curriculum

Train while you are away

Collect → train → evaluate → keep or restore → advance.

Sixty stages cover ten skill tracks. After completion, fresh-seed review rounds run automatically, retraining skills that need practice. Sessions renew automatically; resource and failure safeguards still pause safely. Navigation advances at 90%; broader skills at 80%, each on two complete passing tests (three tasks per bot). A saved model that already passes is confirmed on fresh trials before advancing. Candidates that score below the previous model are restored. Cycles renew every 112 cycles or 6 hours. You can pause at any time; stalled or unsafe runs still pause for review.

Additional controls and system information

Loading system information...

Testing does not update the network. Tests scale with the configured bot count: three tasks per bot. Training uses the examples already collected and leaves the bots waiting when it finishes.

What to expect

  1. Collect examplesThe teacher demonstrates the current task. Their observations and actions become training examples. The network is not learning yet.
  2. Train the networkThe bots stop moving on purpose. The network studies your examples for 100 training passes, then saves a checkpoint.
  3. Test learned behaviorThe bots move using the trained network, without the teacher. Watch how many goals they reach.
  4. Review and repeatCompare the results. More examples may help, but a perfect score on this simple course calls for a harder task.

The curricula cover bounded navigation and survival foundations. General survival, crafting and combat remain future work.
