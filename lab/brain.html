All input and hidden values come from a sampled forward pass, at most twice per second per watched bot. Lines show the three strongest input weights per hidden neuron and eight strongest hidden weights per output; other connections are hidden for readability. Softmax bars are relative scores, not calibrated success probabilities. Biases are included in inference but not drawn. Teacher mode can return a different action from the model’s preference. Final movement may also be changed by controller routines; this view does not yet trace every downstream override. No new sample while sleeping or performing scripted work is normal.
