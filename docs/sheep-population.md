# Larger maintained sheep population

The trial now maintains a cap of 32 white adult sheep and checks replenishment
every 60 seconds, starting 30 seconds after deployment onto the restored terrain.
Up to eight successful sheep births are allowed in one wave. Sheep still must be
found, approached and gathered normally; no wool or food goes into inventories.

Each wave checks up to 16 explored pasture locations and 24 regional samples on
an eight-block grid. Actual grass, two empty headroom cells, eight-block player
clearance, six-block sheep spacing and exclusion from house plots remain required.
The unprepared-world pasture fix is included. Population counts and successful
births are acknowledged from Minecraft and exposed in the world session status.

Cow, pig and chicken caps remain 12, 8 and 12. Their existing bounded replacement
checks run on the same one-minute interval. Reserved assessment trials continue
to exclude replenishment. Changing these environment rules starts a fresh model
comparison, retaining demonstrations and restoring any unvalidated model bundle.
