# Combat foundations: stages 141–150

Ten appended stages preserve the numbering and saved progress of the existing 140 courses. They introduce equipping wooden/stone/iron swords, wearing armor, timed attacks against a stationary zombie, fighting a moving zombie and husk, recovering spacing, two enemies, pursuit around cover, off-hand shields, a skeleton, and a mixed three-monster room. Separate training/testing arenas and seeded starts remain in use.

Combat is an independent 82→128→7 CPU policy (11,527 parameters); existing policies retain their architecture and weights. Seven intentions are approach, aim left, aim right, back away, guard/wait, equip gear, attack. Twelve combat-specific features encode target presence/range/bearing, equipment readiness, attack cooldown, line of sight, close pressure, remaining targets, health and shield readiness. A bounded sensor finds named training mobs in the assigned lane; the policy receives target bearings, just as navigation receives a goal bearing. This is assisted combat sensing, not autonomous discovery of arbitrary entities across a survival world.

The teacher equips inventory items via the same actuator available to the student, approaches and aims at the monster torso, attacks no faster than once every 650 ms, uses safe backpedaling at close range and guards between strikes when a shield is equipped. Attack actuators check permission, ownership label, monster type, lane bounds, visibility, reach and held weapon. Players and unrelated monsters cannot be selected. Combat controls are explicitly enabled by combat_practice in curriculum-permissions.json. No client mod, new library, GPU, extra neural actions for older skills or OP privileges for training bots are required.

Real summoned zombies, husks and skeletons are persistent and tagged lab_combat, without loot or item pickup. Enclosed glass walls and a transparent roof bound each lane while keeping the streaming camera view clear. Existing high opaque practice-area roofs prevent daylight burning. Ambient protection still clears unrelated hostiles but exempts the tagged opponents. Reset removes the previous lane's monsters. Pausing, manual mode changes, candidate training practice changes and completed trials remove combat mobs, so idle bots cannot be attacked during analysis. These tagged mobs must not be spawned outside owned arenas.

Success requires the assigned weapon and armor (and shield when required), surviving within bounds above the stage health floor, and observed deaths of the required opponents after the bot attacked those specific entities. Attack attempts alone cannot pass a fight. Trial timeouts and low health fail rather than skipping the stage. Normal two-batch 80% promotion gates, non-regression candidate acceptance and automatic refresher training apply. This introduces bounded basic PvE mechanics; it does not claim general combat competence, ranged weapon use, learned critical hits, crafting or full survival autonomy.

Focused verification: tests/test_combat.py and tests/combat.test.js cover progression preservation, tagged containment, equipment and survival gates, observation shape, cooldown, target restrictions, line of sight, death deduplication and safe reverse controls. Native teacher results are recorded separately under docs/locomotion-combat-*.json; teacher success demonstrates course feasibility, not student learning.

Initial native teacher validation: 79/80 successful trials across all ten courses with eight bots. Every course passed 8/8 except pursuit around cover (7/8); that failure crossed the health floor and was correctly recorded as a failure. Existing combat policy weights were not trained during these reference runs; demonstrations were collected for later learning.

Final checks: 105 Python tests and 73 Node tests passed. After adding per-trial opponent cleanup, equipment and the mixed monster room passed another 16/16 teacher trials. Teacher-free existing navigation and wooden-door regression checks passed 32/32. Combat room ceilings are glass so the streaming camera can see the fights from above. Native results are kept in locomotion-combat-intro-teacher.json, locomotion-combat-fights-teacher.json, locomotion-combat-final-teacher.json and locomotion-combat-regression-student.json.

Protection verification after reported missing opponents: a tagged, invulnerable stationary zombie survived 22 seconds in an elevated protected area, while an untagged control zombie was removed. Ambient protection was left enabled. See combat-protection-native.json. Stages 141 and 142 intentionally spawn no monsters; stage 143 introduces the stationary zombie. Dashboard messages now make equipment-only stages explicit. The curriculum was rewound to 141 without changing model weights or history.
