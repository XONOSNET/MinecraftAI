# Repeated First Night stalls

Saved reports in `logs/stuck-again-before.json` showed four stopped bots and a blocked return-home route during teacher practice. The service error log was empty. Automatic learning was paused and pending model restoration settled before changing the controller.

Confirmed corrections:

- SolarSwift's route expanded one node and could not leave its plot's outer strip. Private-area exclusion permitted only the inner room, even when the bot was already standing inside the larger forbidden plot footprint. The occupied plot is now permitted for departure; other private plots remain excluded. Native replay went from stationary for the full 8.5-second test to reaching the target in 5.196 seconds.
- AmberRobin's long homeward route was clipped by the 96-cell search box. Its approach was 96 cells away in Z and needed space beyond that to turn around terrain near camp. Radius now scales with destination distance, capped at 192, with the existing 8,192-node budget. The full native return completed in 50.401 seconds with recorded neighboring-player perception. Removing other players from the old route did not solve it, so player clearance was preserved.
- OpalWren was stranded at Y59 among stone overhangs at Y61. At that position the resource scan found 61 log coordinates, 47 passing the existing hazard filter, but no selected reachable tree. First Night stone mining had allowed neighboring blocks below the bot's feet, removing support and creating pockets beneath overhangs. This exposed-face lesson now excludes those lower blocks, including cached candidates. It still mines stone at feet level and above. No tunnel or free escape/resource commands were added.
- DuskMoth's temporary-column removal stopped after a single failed dig precheck. Full native build/mining/descent passed both before and after the change, so the exact original refusal remains unconfirmed. Descent now keeps the tracked support, waits two ticks and permits three bounded attempts. It never pops an unremoved block or counts a failed attempt as progress. A persistent refusal reports support position/material, visibility, reach and dig eligibility.

OakToad's saved gather route and BirchToad's step-down route completed in replay; their transient behavior was not represented as a separately proven defect. Unit tests cover plot departure with other houses protected, routes beyond 96 cells, transient and persistent column refusal, and preserved lower stone support. All 280 JavaScript tests passed.

Native evidence: `stuck-again-replay-before.json`, `stuck-again-replay-native.json`, `stuck-return-home-native.json`, `stuck-resource-inspect.json`, `stuck-column-native.json`.
