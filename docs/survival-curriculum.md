# World survival curriculum

Select a sequence in **Lesson sequence** on the dashboard, then start automatic
learning or a manual trial. The in-game world menu also offers iron, homestead
and defense lessons. Automatic learning advances after two cycles with at least
75% complete student sequences. An unsuccessful candidate is still rolled back.

| Sequence | Verified additions |
| --- | --- |
| Iron expedition (20 milestones) | Finish the stone base; descend five reversible terraces; mine coal and iron with the stone pickaxe; climb back and return home; smelt eight iron; craft an iron pickaxe, shears and sword. |
| Homestead (26 milestones) | Mine and smelt 32 iron; shear three white sheep; collect wool and craft/place a two-block bed; obtain beef from lesson livestock; cook food; craft/equip all four iron armor pieces; store food and spare iron in the chest. |
| Home defense (29 milestones) | Complete the homestead; encounter one tagged, weaker husk outside; retreat inside and close the door; defeat the owned threat with the combat specialist; return home safely. |

The verified bed becomes the bot's home and Minecraft respawn point. A floating
bed icon and the bot's generated name mark it above the hut. Home remains
registered until the simulation resets. Return goals lead to a walkable interior
position beside the bed. New trials clear old markers and lesson animals.

Stations contain real blocks and animals, created once per bot per trial.
There are 40 iron ore and eight coal ore blocks, allowing a margin for collection.
The staircase and catch floor are prepared, so this version teaches descending,
mining, collecting and climbing home; it does not yet teach excavating a staircase.
The expanded restore area covers these stations and their five-block depth.
Early trials keep a 20-minute safety deadline; survival sequences allow 40 minutes.
Slow but progressing resource trips are not culled for speed.

Navigation, gathering/mining, shelter approach and combat select specialist
policies in student runs. Teacher runs record matching decisions, and combat now
joins the protected train/evaluate/restore bundle. Recipes, block placement,
smelting, animal harvesting, armor equipping and storage remain scripted helpers
in both modes. This is a growing task curriculum, not evidence that crafting or
construction has become a learned policy.

Normal monster protection remains enabled. Only explicitly tagged lesson threats
are exempt, and they are removed when their bot finishes, stops or the trial resets.
Defense requires a registered bed home and is reached after armor and provisioning.

Native verification uses an isolated sky fixture. Its starting stone base and
24 armor ingots are granted only to shorten the test; eight iron are actually
mined and smelted, and all subsequent recipes/actions use server confirmations.
Production homestead runs must gather and smelt their full 32 iron.
