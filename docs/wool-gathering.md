# Wool gathering: useful access versus raw sheep count

A live server-side census counted 23 sheep, including 19 with Age=0, white wool and no shearing flag. This establishes live supply, not guaranteed usable supply for every bot. In a geometry-only check from camp, four of 16 tracked eligible sheep had complete reversible dry-land approaches through the client's loaded terrain. The other checks exhausted their reachable graph without hitting the enlarged 32,768-node diagnostic budget. This does not prove permanent geographic isolation or account for swimming; it demonstrates that increasing sheep counts alone is insufficient under the current loaded-terrain dry-land rules. Positions and visibility change as sheep wander.

The wool search also penalized revisited locations by 35 points per visit but gave remembered resource goals only a 12-point preference. Thus known sheep areas could lose to unknown terrain. Remembered food/wool patches now rank ahead of unknown frontier cells regardless of previous visit count, while rejected and recently depleted destinations still retain their cooldowns. Known approaches are considered nearest first.

Population maintenance already adds animals during natural training. Sheep checks now prefer grass candidates beside routes bots actually traversed near camp, selected from existing bounded road-memory reports. Sites must be within 12–48 blocks of camp, near its elevation, inside the reserved area, spaced apart and outside house plots. Native commands recheck five grass support blocks, two air blocks, and eight-block player clearance at the actual recorded height before spawning. The whole-area cap remains 24 sheep and the wave budget remains at most two successful sheep additions per 120-second check. Up to eight conditional candidates are tried, with a success counter preventing extra additions. Other livestock retain their existing rules; assessment trials still receive no replenishment. If no walked candidates exist, the existing regional-grass routine remains in use. These are conditional animal spawns, not inventory grants or credited milestones.

The inspected past trial had 64 candidate road locations; the loaded-world check found 28 passing the conservative grass/air screen. This placement policy improves access opportunities without changing terrain or enabling unsafe water crossing.

Natural hunting reports now include carried white wool, nearby adult white sheep, eligible candidates, remembered patches and rejected targets. The dashboard shows those separately so a nearby sheep count is not confused with a proven reachable target, and indicates when refills use walked-route candidates.

Validation: 263 Python tests and 293 JavaScript tests passed. Native `verify_walked_pasture_native.cjs` used an isolated grass platform: first wave spawned two sheep, second brought the total to four, another stayed capped at four, and replacing grass with unsuitable stone produced zero. Diagnostic animals, items, blocks and operator permission were removed. The census script only read production entities/terrain and did not add production resources.

Results are in `wool-census-summary.json` and `walked-pasture-native.json`; test logs are under `logs/wool-*`. Learning was paused and its unvalidated bundle restored before updating. The lab restarted to load Python changes, then resumes First Night level 1, scenario 2, size 128 with a fresh baseline and candidate under the same controller/resource rules. Checkpoints, training data, identities, configuration and unrelated edits were preserved. Whether this improves full policy completion still requires the new measured runs.
