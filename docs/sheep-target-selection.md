# Opportunistic sheep selection

Natural First Night hunting previously retained a distant animal lock, used terrain-coordinate rejections to filter moving animals, and treated nearby players/mining reservations as ownership of the animal. A failed approach deferred an animal for 30 seconds regardless of later movement. Those filters could leave a bot scouting while an otherwise usable sheep was nearby.

The new selector checks loaded animals and explicit supported-station ownership, white/adult/unsheared wool eligibility, trial boundaries and current sightlines. A substantially closer visible reachable animal can replace a distant or hidden lock; close active hunts stay stable. Target selection cancels the previous scouting goal and releases its exploration reservation. Terrain-coordinate rejection no longer hides live animals. Failed animal approaches use an eight-second deferral, invalidated early if the sheep moves two blocks or the hunter moves three blocks. Routes remain checked against real terrain and player clearance.

Animal reservations now refer to the animal identity and an actual current hunter, rather than an unrelated nearby player. Claims expire after three seconds without renewal. Selection is bounded to three candidate animals and three approach routes per candidate; successful approach plans are briefly cached and invalidated by movement. It does not supply animals or award progress.

Livestock reports include counts of skipped animals and a selection reason. The dashboard wool status renders the reason; wool searches also append it to activity when sheep are loaded but not selected.

Validation: 320 JavaScript tests passed. The native two-sheep fixture confirmed that a nearby sheep replaced a distant selected sheep even with a stale rejected terrain coordinate, cancelled scouting, equipped the carried axe and killed the selected sheep in two actual hits (1.39 seconds). The fixture was cleaned up and did not write production learning data. See docs/sheep-selection-native.json.

Deployment: the existing bounded rollout helper is running in exec session 88942, watching evaluation world-1793357794889018100. Its report logs/floor-fix-rollout.json confirms waiting_for_completed_comparison. The current trial was not stopped. After that comparison is recorded, the helper waits for a fresh baseline terrain reset, pauses preparation, restarts only the bot controller, checks eight fresh connections and resumes a new comparison using the current saved options. This also loads the saved floor recovery fix. The helper exits instead if learning is disabled by the user. The selector is not active until that restart; the dashboard formatting update is available on browser refresh, and will show reasons once new controller reports arrive.
