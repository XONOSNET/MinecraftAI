# Opening attack replication · training only

Use the [original opening protocol](combat-opening-protocol.md) for eligibility, controls, the frozen saved tensor, observation and action semantics, the 50 ms ambiguity rule, outcome accounting, and the safety gate. The [pilot](combat-opening-result.md) stays separate. Its fights supply no rows or labels to this replication.

Run 12 fresh matched zombie starts in order, `12668833, 12760363, 12034427, 12100099, 12057153, 12707889, 12270827, 12480339, 12906348, 12097883, 12074184, 12094710`. These are `random.Random(170212).sample(range(12_000_000, 13_000_000), 12)`. Each start runs both arms and alternates control-first and opening-first, beginning control-first. Budget 24 encounters and 18 minutes, checked between fights. The same isolated Normal arena, adult zombie, iron loadout, and native combat checks apply. The opening arm attempts exactly one substitution, then uses the frozen saved policy. No retries of invalid starts or of fights after release.

The native dashboard request gets up to three attempts of 1.5 seconds each, with up to 250 ms between attempts, only when a request times out and before release. Verify the same player and target, frozen state, loadout and pose against the reset snapshot throughout and immediately before release. Exhausted timeouts, invalid setup, unhealthy response, or infrastructure failure stop the block and require verified cleanup. Maintain the existing server/PID, persistence, RAM, ready and lifecycle limits. Record the health attempts, timing, arm order, exclusions, and per-fight traces.

Pass only with all 12 pairs and at least 3 net additional first hits (+25 points) after giving control the benefit of ambiguity. Require deaths, escapes, and mean gross damage at or below control, and wins at or above control. Use only the replication to apply the gate. If incomplete, mark the gate unevaluated. If it fails, distinguish refusal, already-late proposal, delayed request, unconfirmed swing, and confirmed hit with later damage before deciding whether further training is warranted.

If it passes, derive at most one labeled opening observation per complete replication start. Use only first-eligible observations which changed a saved nonattack proposal. Assign attack a positive label if the opening has an attributable first hit, the control unambiguously did not land first, and no paired damage/win/death/escape regression follows. Assign a negative label for a clear control first hit and an opening failure, or for unambiguous failures in both with no safety advantage and no attributable first-hit benefit. Leave ambiguous cases, missing windows, and otherwise tied cases unlabeled. Treat labels as matched outcome preferences across stochastic trajectories. Require at least two starts per label before fitting.

Fit deterministic float64 logistic regression to `(range_blocks−2.7)/0.3`, `abs(relative_bearing)/π`, and `clamp(saved_attack_logit−max(saved_nonattack_logits), −20, 0)/20` from the first eligible observation. Use unweighted mean binary cross-entropy plus half the sum of squared coefficients, including intercept. Initialize at zero and make 200 full-batch gradient updates at learning rate 0.1. Retain the saved policy everywhere else. At that opening, the fitted head selects attack only if it predicts more than 50% probability, with no forced attack. Evaluate once on 8 fresh development starts sampled with `random.Random(170213).sample(range(13_000_000, 14_000_000), 8)`: `13300130, 13278258, 13434558, 13656125, 13306912, 13168775, 13905289, 13817830`. Compare saved, one-time rule, and learned in cyclic arm order, same starts and rules, within 18 minutes checked between fights. Require at least two net conservative first-hit gains over saved, no first-hit loss or safety regression relative to the one-time rule, no safety regression relative to saved, and at least two decisions changed from saved with at least one eligible decline of the forced rule. No extra exploratory fights. A pass allows preregistration of a fresh held-out comparison. The reserved holdouts stay sealed throughout this task.
