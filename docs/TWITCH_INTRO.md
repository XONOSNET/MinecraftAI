Hey everyone, I'm xonosnet, and we're building a Minecraft AI training lab live on stream.

These eight bots learn from demonstrations, then get tested using their own small neural networks. We keep models that perform well and roll back updates that make them worse. Passing once is not enough: they must succeed again on fresh trials before advancing.

The curriculum builds from navigation, gathering, food and shelter to bridges, real doors and hazard courses. We're expanding it to 60 stages. When the curriculum is complete, it automatically begins review rounds so forgotten skills get tested again.

We can follow the bots with cameras, cycle between them, and see actions, trial counts and reset timers. Those labels report controller state, not generated inner thoughts.

This is a training experiment, not a finished survival AI. The interesting part is watching where it succeeds, where it gets stuck, and whether the next version actually improves.
