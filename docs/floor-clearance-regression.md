# Floor placement regression after construction clearing

Inspection of baseline world-1793453189494087000 found six stopped bots. Four stopped on `floor: Cannot safely clear construction obstruction grass_block/large_fern`; one could not return to its plot, and another had a stationary gathering/navigation failure. Minecraft was healthy at 20 TPS, 12.9ms MSPT. These floor failures were controller errors introduced by the centralized construction clearer, not evidence that the policy needed more examples.

The placement helper's center ray to a floor hole can cross adjacent supporting ground below house-floor height or vegetation outside the house volume. It attempted to clear those cells and correctly refused its own unsafe excavation. It now clears sightline obstructions only inside the owned building volume. Neighboring ground and plants remain intact; the normal placement supporting-face selection handles floor holes. Unsafe/inaccessible construction clearing in the floor workflow also uses its existing bounded alternative-stance recovery instead of ending the bot immediately.

Floor-work stance selection additionally leaves enough space for navigation's arrival tolerance. Stopping short of an adjacent cell center can otherwise leave the bot over the tile it must excavate, repeatedly accepting the same already-arrived stance. Work stances now remain clear of the dig footprint even when movement stops 0.4m short.

Validation: 335 JavaScript tests pass. Regressions reproduce a floor ray through adjacent foundation, vegetation outside the owned volume, and safe stance clearance with arrival tolerance. A complete isolated survival build replaced all 25 grass floor tiles with actual planks in 45.106 seconds, reached the next `table` phase, preserved adjacent grass and the outside large fern, and retained full health. Its first diagnostic attempt reached 23 tiles without completing within 75 seconds; the complete workflow was checked again after the stance correction. Diagnostic terrain and supplies were limited to a temporary separate fixture, removed afterward. No trial successes, resources, model updates or replay samples were fabricated. Evidence: logs/full-floor-native.json.

The two distant-route/gathering failures are separate and remain unresolved by this floor change. Deployment restores any unvalidated model bundle and begins a new comparison with the same updated controller on both sides.
