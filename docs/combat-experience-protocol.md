# Combat experience experiment · protocol v1

**Hypothesis:** One 32-fight outcome update schedule can yield a candidate that beats both the frozen native teacher and the original saved combat checkpoint. The saved arm is a no-update control. All arms share the same observations, movement, equipment, critical-hit helper and restrictions; no assist ablation or new inputs.

**Before any holdout:** Run one training zombie and one matched shielded skeleton smoke (three arms). Require synchronized starts, valid terminal rows, trace coverage, realized target damage/motion, changed tensors, reload-equivalent logits, and verified cleanup. This checks execution, not improvement. The earlier zombie batches were 72/72 wins, but reference mean damage was 2.40–2.96 HP, leaving room for the existing 1 HP margin. Keep the legacy arena and equipment. A train-only feasibility check requires some realized target damage and at least two proposed actions with distinct effects before scoring a holdout. If even damage is at floor, stop and preregister fresh starts with a benchmark-only armor variation.

**Frozen design:** Paper 1.21.4, Normal, verified 20 health/food, iron sword/full iron, shield only on skeletons, one native opponent. Use the existing glass arena and seed-to-pose formulas. Freeze controller sources, behavior defaults, fixture source, reference weights, learner recipe and ordered start lists before collecting experience. Novel start IDs are in separate seed domains from historical runs; use preregistered smoke seed 70201 and initial full seed 70202. Block by start and rotate arm order within each batch. Matching geometry does not make Minecraft mob AI identical. Repeated episodes on this server are not independent world replications.

**Update:** Existing clipped outcome learner: eight batches of four zombie fights, temperature 1.2, Adam 0.0005, four gradient passes per batch, clip ratio 0.8–1.2, reference KL coefficient 0.05, gradient norm cap 1, discount 0.99. Keep the original reward (−0.002 per decision, −0.08/HP of damage, terminal +2 win / −3 death / −1 other), applied to acknowledged fight outcomes. Record proposals, consumed controls, helper acceptance, attack requests and native target-hurt packets separately. Last two do not imply one-to-one attribution. No explicit probability mask; physical/interaction constraints are shared. Do not tune on holdout results.

**Endpoint and gates:** In each of two fresh 12-start zombie batches, candidate must have no more deaths or fewer wins than either reference, plus more paired wins or at least 1 HP better shared-win mean damage, with the existing nominal one-sided sign test at 0.05. Shielded skeleton sentinel: same no-death/no-win-regression checks and mean damage at most 1 HP worse than either reference. Report all deaths, time/step limits, escapes, proposal/effect distributions, paired differences and descriptive uncertainty. Time is secondary and compared only for matched wins with equivalent damage. No victory inference from a smoke or incomplete batch.

**Budget and stopping:** One CPU thread and one experimental player. Initial full budget: 32 training fights + up to 108 scored/native holdout fights, 75 minutes wall time checked between encounters; no unbounded retries. A setup/infra failure or invalid start (unverified pose/loadout/release, predecision damage or drift, excessive start latency, disconnect, server operation or loss of monitoring) is recorded and aborts this run. Never score an incomplete matched group or selectively rerun one arm; a later continuation must replay the entire group or use a new preregistered block. Stop for production health/intentional shutdown, not for fixture low health or hunger. If either reference fails the completed first zombie batch, the conjunctive promotion gate is already impossible: stop for futility, leave the second zombie and skeleton holdouts unspent, and make no sentinel or promotion claim.

**Retention:** A qualifying champion is local only. Save tensors, optimizer and configuration and preserve original references and source hashes. Two cycles on disjoint new training and evaluation starts use the same recipe (up to 32 training and 252 evaluation fights per cycle, 120 minutes checked between encounters). Protect against the current champion and keep the original edge; otherwise roll back. Reload the selected checkpoint, verify tensor identity and inference, and assess it on an additional fresh matched block against the original references. If that block fails, stop the retention experiment and retain the prior checkpoint without claiming that it passed the fresh block.

**Transfer:** Untested. Only an arena success warrants a separately preregistered reserved-site wilderness test at unchanged support, with both arms on the same controller. Measure sequence completion, surviving encounters, successful retreats, stalls and lesson progress. No arena checkpoint goes to production.
