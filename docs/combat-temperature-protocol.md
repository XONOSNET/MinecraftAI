# Combat temperature ablation · training only

Replay the 32 archived training fights from `combat-experience-v1-full`, stopping before the evaluation records. Compare a 2×2 factorial: reference KL temperature 1.0 (original) or 1.2 (matched to exploration), crossed with logged or zero reward. Keep the same frozen original tensors, fresh Adam state, observed actions/order, eight batches of four trajectories, gradient passes, loss weights, discount and inference argmax. Verify the original real-reward arm exactly reproduces the archived candidate. With matched temperatures the zero-reward arm should be at the reference fixed point in exact arithmetic; report floating-point drift rather than rounding it away.

**Offline gate, set before replay:** Measure a signed shift into spacing/wait (actions 3 or 4) in visible, fully iron-equipped, aligned (absolute angle ≤0.12 rad), within 3 blocks, during positive sword cooldown. An opportunity counts as a new protective intent only when matched real-reward proposes 3/4 and none of original real, original zero and matched zero do. Subtract reverse cases where matched real alone leaves 3/4. Require a net shift of at least 10 percentage points, newly protective choices from at least four fights, and no reduction of more than 5 percentage points in attack proposals at aligned visible range with cooldown zero versus original real. These are practical screening margins. Report exact changes by range, cooldown (>400 ms versus wind-up window) and alignment even if the gate fails. Off-policy archive decisions are not evidence of executed behavior or safety.

**Conditional native probe:** Only if the offline gate passes, use four matched zombie starts and four matched shielded skeleton starts (32 fights, four arms), rotating arm order. Draw four starts per mob in the 8-million and 9-million domains using Python Random(70210).sample in that order. These are training-only starts, disjoint from previous data and sealed blocks. Use the frozen v1 arena, difficulty, loadout, motor, start checks and accounting. One bot, one CPU thread, 20 minutes checked between starts; stop on an invalid encounter, production health issue or loss of monitoring. Do not substitute a single arm after an invalid start. Measure back ticks and stationary wait ticks (action 4 without horizontal controls on an observed physics tick); use held-item ticks on skeletons for shield-use intent, not confirmed damage blocking. Compare execution in the same visible, near-range cooldown window. Require at least two additional back/held-shield ticks or two additional stationary wait ticks versus every control on at least four distinct starts, no more deaths or fewer wins, and no higher mean damage within each mob type versus any control. A passing screen warrants a separate preregistration with new seeds and cannot spend the sealed v1 blocks.

**If the gate fails:** Stop temperature tuning. Use only the archived training trace to locate the first observed intake damage relative to first in-range, cooldown-ready decision, first attack proposal/request and first observed target hurt. Separate earlier potential opportunities from same-window and late events. Quantify what was proposed and executed before the first damage. The trace cannot prove that range or sight held at packet time or that a counterfactual choice would have prevented a hit. Do not run an additional live ablation to chase the result.
